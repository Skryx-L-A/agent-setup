# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Please open an issue first for larger changes, so the approach can
be agreed on before you invest time.

## Contributor License Agreement

This project is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and is also offered
under commercial licenses (see [COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)). To keep both
possible, every contributor accepts the [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) once. You keep
the copyright in your contribution.

To accept it, comment on your pull request with exactly this sentence:

> I have read the Contributor License Agreement and I agree to it.

A check named `cla` confirms the acceptance. Pull requests cannot be merged while it fails.
After your first accepted pull request you are listed in
[CLA-SIGNATURES.md](CLA-SIGNATURES.md) and do not need to comment again in this repository.

If you contribute as part of your job or for a client, your employer or client must agree. Write
to **licensing@prontea.de** for the Corporate Contributor License Agreement.
